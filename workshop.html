Build your first AI agent

Over five short exercises you'll turn a plain chatbot into an event-planning agent called Pip. Each exercise adds one building block, and you see the effect right away.

This is a demo simulation

Pip's answers are produced by scripted rules on this page, not by an AI model. They still react to what you type: change the budget, remove a rule or switch off the skill, and the answer changes. No AI account and no cost.

Want to see a real model? Use Copy prompt and paste it into any AI assistant to compare.

The mission

Your team of 12 wants a one-day offsite in November. By the end, Pip will check the team calendar, search venues, recommend one in your house format, and draft the invite. It will never book anything without asking you first.

02Job description · 15 min

Give your agent a job description

A system prompt is written once and shapes every answer. Send the tricky request, then delete the "Never book" line and send it again. It's the job description a new hire reads on day one.

03Skills · 15 min

Teach it a skill

A skill is a saved recipe card for one job, done your way. Run once with the skill on and once with it off. Then delete a line from the skill and watch that part disappear. The assistant pulls the card out of a drawer only when the task needs it.

04Tools · 15 min

Give it hands

Now Pip can use three tools: check the team calendar, search venues and draft an invite. Watch the tool log to see each step. Try changing the budget or removing "outdoor" from the task. Tools turn an advisor into an assistant who can actually do things.
